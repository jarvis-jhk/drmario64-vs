## Decisions

- Modes: VS offers NORMAL / FLASH / TIME ATTACK for two humans; all three are in
  scope (FLASH and TA differ only in setup and win rule). Wins needed 1/2/3 (default 3)
  becomes a VS-screen option since we have no Options menu.
- Sound: SFX extracted at first run from the user's ROM (three uncompressed audio banks,
  offsets in SPEC part 03 §4). The decomp's audio library is NOT MIT, so the N64 ADPCM
  decoder and effect player are our own, written from public format documentation.
- Win/draw jingles are music sequences in the original. Jan wants no music: skip them
  for v0.1; revisit if the round end feels empty.
